## Detection
- IMPORTANT: if the user asks for a crossword, crossword puzzle, word puzzle,
  clue/trivia grid, fill-in puzzle, or any across-and-down word game — with or
  without multiplayer/co-op/versus — set app_category to "crossword".
- Keywords: "crossword", "word puzzle", "clue", "across", "down", "trivia grid",
  "fill in", "vocabulary puzzle", "themed crossword".
- Examples: "space crossword", "geography crossword", "movie trivia crossword",
  "science vocabulary crossword", "multiplayer crossword", "co-op word puzzle".
